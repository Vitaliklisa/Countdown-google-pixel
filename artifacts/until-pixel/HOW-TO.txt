Until — countdown for Pixel

This zip is a phone-ready copy of Until. It is not an Android APK
(Play Store package). Open the HTML file in Chrome and it runs like
the app: title, description, future date, and a live years / months /
days / hours countdown.

On a Pixel (or any Android phone)
1. Download and unzip this file.
2. Open Until.html.
3. Chrome will offer to open it. Choose Chrome.
4. Tap Create event, pick a future day, and watch the count.

Add it to your home screen
1. With Until.html open in Chrome, tap the three dots.
2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
3. Until appears as its own icon.

Events are saved on this phone only.
